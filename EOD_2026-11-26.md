## 2. Key Completed Tasks & Features

### A. Multi-HR Separation & Approval Attribution
- **Role Parity & Activity Tracking:** Maintained equal functional capability between HR Head and Sub-HR while ensuring HR Head can inspect audit logs.
- **Approval Attribution:** Every approval/rejection across leaves and reimbursements stamps the exact HR name (`approved_by_name`), making all HR decisions transparent to the entire HR team.
- **Strict HR Invitation Only:** Disabled self-registration for employees; employees can only sign in via HR credentials.

### B. Employee First-Login Onboarding & Credential Management
- **Mandatory First-Login Profile Form:** On initial login with HR-assigned credentials, employees are prompted to enter:
  - Aadhaar Number
  - PAN Number
  - Residential Address
  - Emergency Contact Name & Relationship
  - Date of Birth
- **HR Credential Control:** HR can view employee IDs and assigned passwords, and update them directly. Employees cannot change their passwords.
- **Universal Login:** Enabled authentication using Username, Email, or User ID.

### C. Attendance & Geolocation Simplification
- **Biometric Machine Sync Removal:** Removed all legacy `BIO-PROVISIONAL` code, physical machine sync tasks, and database biometric temporary tables.
- **Flexible Location Punching:** Removed the "Location Requests" feature and restriction checks. Employees can now punch in/out from any location without approval barriers.
- **Clean Slate Database:** Wiped dummy employee data from the database for a clean production start.

### D. Reimbursements & Joining Document Management
- **Employee Reimbursement Flow:** Added employee reimbursement submission supporting Amount, Reason, Payment Method (UPI / Cash), and screenshot/receipt upload.
- **Direct HR Reimbursement Approval:** HR can review receipt attachments and approve/reject reimbursement payments directly.
- **Employee Joining Documents:** Added dedicated section in employee profile records for HR to upload and manage joining documents (PDF, JPG, PNG).

### E. Complete Removal of Document Requests & Employee Documents Section
- **Removed Document Requests Queue from HR Panel:** Completely removed the "Requests" tab, request approval actions, and pending request table from the HR Documents page (`/documents`). HR can now directly generate Appointment Letters, Offer Letters, Salary Slips, Relieving Letters, and Experience Certificates manually.
- **Removed Documents Section from Employee Panel:** Eliminated the Documents page from the employee portal navigation (`EmployeeSidebar.tsx`), removed the Documents tab from employee profile (`MyProfilePage.tsx`), replaced Documents with Expenses in employee Quick Actions (`QuickActionsCard.tsx`), and redirected `/employee/documents` to `/employee/dashboard`. Employees request documents in-person manually as decided by HR.

### F. Automated Email Invitations with Assigned Credentials
- **Instant Invitation Dispatch:** When HR registers a new employee, a professional HTML invitation email is automatically dispatched via SMTP (`info@seawindsolution.in` on port 465 SSL).
- **Direct Login Credentials:** The invitation contains the Employee ID, registered email, username, assigned password, and a direct link to the login portal.
- **First-Time Sign-In Instructions:** The email instructs the employee to log in directly using the assigned credentials and complete their mandatory onboarding details (Aadhaar, PAN, residential address, emergency contact, DOB), noting that passwords are centrally HR-managed.
- **On-Demand Resend Option:** Added an on-demand "Resend Invitation" action button in the HR employee management table (`/api/employees/{id}/resend-invitation`) so HR can re-dispatch credentials anytime.

### G. Official Brand Logo Integration Across All Panels & Email Templates
- **Standardized Asset Storage:** Installed the official SeaWind trademark logo (`SeaWind - REGENERATION OF IT SERVICES`) across all key asset directories (`frontend/public/seawind-logo.png`, `frontend/public/trademark-logo-new.png`, `frontend/public/favicon.png`, `backend/images/seawind_logo.png`).
- **Portal Panels:** Integrated the official logo badge into:
  - Admin & HR main sidebar (`Sidebar.tsx`)
  - Employee portal sidebar (`EmployeeSidebar.tsx`)
  - Top header bars on mobile (`Header.tsx`, `EmployeeHeader.tsx`)
  - Browser favicon (`favicon.png`)
- **Login & Authentication Screens:** Replaced generic placeholders in `AuthLayout.tsx` and added responsive brand headers for mobile sign-in in `LoginPage.tsx`.
- **Email Templates:** Both the Invitation Credentials email and the OTP Login Verification email now feature the official logo header via inline MIME attachments (`cid:seawind_logo`), ensuring guaranteed rendering in all desktop and mobile mail clients.

### H. Calendar Responsiveness & Cropping Resolution
- **Proportional Cell Sizing:** Fixed day cells from rigid elongated pill dimensions (`h-16 sm:h-20`) to balanced square proportions (`h-10 sm:h-11` in compact view).
- **Indicator Dots vs. Truncated Text:** Replaced truncating text strings (`Sunday` -> `Su...`, `2nd/4th Sat` -> `2...`) with crisp color-coded indicator dots (🔴 Sunday, 🟠 2nd/4th Saturday, 🎉 Official Holiday, 🟢 Approved Leave, 🟡 Pending Leave).
- **Collision Fix for Current Day:** Removed colliding inline `"Today"` text next to date numbers; highlighted current date with an indigo circular pill.
- **Dashboard Sidebar Optimization:** Expanded dashboard sidebar column to `360px` / `380px` on desktop view for comfortable widget spacing.
