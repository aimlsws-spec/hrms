--- Slide 2 ---
- Project Profile & Problem Statement
- Project Summary
- A comprehensive Human Resource Management System that leverages artificial intelligence to automate and streamline HR operations. The portal integrates employee management, attendance tracking, leave processing, payroll computation, and intelligent assistance into a unified platform.
- Core Challenge
- Traditional HR systems are time-consuming and error-prone, requiring manual data entry across multiple platforms. Organizations struggle with fragmented information, delayed payroll processing, and inefficient employee queries—leading to reduced productivity and increased operational costs.

--- Slide 3 ---
- Project Objectives & System Overview
- Automate HR Operations
- Eliminate manual processes through intelligent automation of employee records, attendance, and payroll computation
- Centralize Data Management
- Create a unified system for all HR functions with real-time access and data synchronization
- Enhance Decision-Making
- Provide actionable insights through comprehensive reports and analytics dashboards
- Intelligent Assistance
- Deploy AI-powered chat assistant to handle employee queries and support requests 24/7

- Technologies & System Requirements

- Backend: FastAPI, Python 3.11 Database: MySQL 8.0 Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript APIs: RESTful architecture PDF Generation: Report engine AI/ML: Large Language Models

- Payroll Processing
- Automatic salary computation with tax deductions, bonuses, and direct deposits

- Automated creation of offer letters, contracts, and performance reports

- Ticket-based support with escalation matrix and resolution tracking
